First Day

ARL Induction & Quiz

All new students must complete the Animal Research Laboratory (ARL) induction before starting any laboratory work. This is mandatory.

ðŸ“‹
Induction Covers
  • Lab safety rules and emergency procedures
  • Biosafety levels and PPE requirements
  • Arthropod handling and containment
  • Waste disposal and decontamination
  • Lab equipment usage rules
ðŸ“
ARL Quiz
  • Taken after completing induction
  • Tests understanding of safety protocols
  • Must be passed before independent lab work
  • Certificate issued upon passing
  • Keep a copy in your Google Drive folder
Other mandatory training: Biosafety and biosecurity training is conducted by the TIDREC Administrative Office. Please follow instructions from TIDREC admin for training dates, registration, and any required refresher sessions.
